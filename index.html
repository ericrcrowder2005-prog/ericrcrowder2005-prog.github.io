About the Fragify Uploader

The Fragify Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel's owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to publish the channel's own finished videos to the channel, with their titles, descriptions, hashtags, thumbnails and English closed captions.

  • It signs in only to the channel owner's own Google account.
  • It does not ask for, read or store anyone else's data.
  • It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Details of what the tool accesses and keeps are in the privacy policy.
